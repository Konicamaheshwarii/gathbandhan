How it works

  1. 🎯

    List your real goals

    Two to five dreams with honest targets. AI can draft your story and suggest amounts that fit your guest list.

  2. 💬

    Share it the Indian way

    One tap to WhatsApp with a ready message, plus a printable QR card for the shagun table at the venue.

  3. 🪔

    Guests bless by UPI

    ₹501, ₹1,100, ₹2,100 or any amount, paid from GPay, PhonePe or Paytm straight to you. No gateway, no fees.

Not a fundraiser. Just shagun, made useful.

  • UPI first. A real upi://pay link and QR code. Money never passes through this site.
  • Blessing wall. Every guest leaves a message; amounts stay private unless you choose to show them.
  • You stay in control. Contributions show as "guest confirmed" until you tick them off against your bank app.
  • Thank-you notes in your language. AI drafts a personal note per guest in English, हिन्दी or Hinglish, ready for WhatsApp.
