Money and privacy

Shagun never handles money. Guests pay with a standard UPI intent link or QR code, straight from their UPI app to the couple's UPI ID. "I've paid" is the guest's own confirmation, which is why the dashboard lets the couple verify each one against their bank app. Wedding pages and contributions are stored in this browser (localStorage); share links carry the wedding page itself so they open on any phone.
